About

Grounding decisions in data — and research questions in the same rigor.

MD Habibul Islam is a Business Analytics graduate (BBA, United International University, Dhaka, 2026) currently working as a Business Analyst Intern at Anwar Group of Industries, applying quantitative research methods to operational data — cleaning, analyzing, and turning it into KPI dashboards and management reports built in Power BI, Excel, and SQL.

Alongside his analyst work, he is an independent researcher in development economics. His study on household food insecurity in Bangladesh — regression-based econometric models applied to 500+ household observations — was published in the International Journal of Public Policy and Administration Research. Two further manuscripts, on AI's employment impact and ESG challenges in the textile sector, are in preparation.

His research interests center on development economics, household food security, and applied econometrics — with a growing focus on causal inference, panel data econometrics, and impact evaluation for evidence-based policy research.
